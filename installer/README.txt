OMNI - a workshop of AI makers that runs on your own computer

To install: double-click "Install Omni.cmd" and answer its questions.

It needs Windows 10 or 11. If Python or Ollama are missing it offers to install them with
winget. Nothing needs administrator rights. Omni then opens at http://localhost:7777 and its
Models page suggests models that suit this computer's video card and memory.

Your projects are kept in %USERPROFILE%\.omni. "Uninstall Omni" in the Start menu removes
the program and asks before touching your projects.
